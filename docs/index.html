See where your day actually goes

RM Activity is a free Mac app that notes which app, web page and window you work in, every five seconds, and turns it into your day: time per category, per client, in meetings, and the week. Everything stays on your Mac.

curl -fsSL https://rmmarketingmcc.github.io/rm-activity/install.sh | bash

Paste it in Terminal. macOS 13 or newer, Apple silicon or Intel. Needs Apple's Command Line Tools; the installer tells you if they are missing. Run the same line again to update.

A live demo with made-up clients. On your Mac it sits in the menu bar.

What you get

Today in the menu bar

Your active time next to the RM mark. Click it, or open the app, for the whole day: what you are on right now, a timeline, and the week.

Real meeting time

A call counts from the microphone: Meet, Zoom, Teams, Slack, WhatsApp or a phone call through the Mac, whatever window is in front, also while you only listen. Dictation never counts.

Time per client

Add your clients to the settings file. A stretch belongs to a client when their name or domain shows up in the page, the address or the window title.

A full page and a widget

Day and week view with sites, pages, apps and a log. Put the panel on your desktop or keep it floating on top.

Private by design

It notes

  • The app in front, every 5 seconds
  • Chrome: the site, the path and the tab title (never the part after ? or #)
  • Other apps: the window title, if you allow Accessibility
  • Which app uses the microphone, never the sound

It never

  • Looks at incognito windows
  • Reads Safari pages (app time only)
  • Counts time away: 3 minutes without keyboard or mouse stops the clock
  • Sends anything anywhere. No account, no cloud, no tracking of you

Safe to install

  • Open source: every line is on GitHub, and the app is built from exactly that code.
  • No admin password, no account, no internet: the app never sends anything anywhere, not even analytics or update checks.
  • The installer checks the download against its fingerprint before it installs anything.
  • The app only runs the exact tracking code it shipped with. If anything changes that file, it stops, so no other program can borrow the permissions you give it.
  • Both permissions are optional, and you can take them back any time in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  • It is not signed by Apple (we are not a registered Apple developer), which is why it installs from Terminal instead of a download. Removing it takes one line, further down.

Your clients and categories

Everything is in one file: ~/Library/Application Support/RM Activity/settings.json. Changes apply to old days too.

"clients": {
  "list": [
    {"name": "Acme Dental", "aliases": ["acme"], "domains": ["acmedental.com"]},
    {"name": "Northwind Plumbing", "domains": ["northwind.co"]}
  ]
}

Make it your own

Open source under the MIT license: a small Swift app and one Python file. Change the categories, the panel, or what it counts. Remove it again with:

curl -fsSL https://rmmarketingmcc.github.io/rm-activity/install.sh | bash -s -- --uninstall
