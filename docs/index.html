Your day and your week

Today's time sits in the menu bar. Open the app for what you are on right now, a timeline of the day, and the week at a glance.

Real meeting time

Counted from the microphone: Meet, Zoom, Teams, Slack, WhatsApp, or a phone call through the Mac. Whatever window is in front, and also while you only listen.

Time per client

Add your clients once. A minute belongs to a client when their name or domain shows up in the page, the web address or the window title.

Every site, page and app

A full page with day and week view: which sites and pages, which apps and files, and a log of every stretch. Keep the window on top, or pin it to your desktop.

Meetings, finally counted right

Most trackers only see the window in front. In a call you share your screen, check a mail, open the client's site, and the meeting quietly disappears from your day. RM Activity watches which app uses the microphone, so every minute of a call counts. Dictation apps never do.

Safe to install

It sees a lot of your day, so it is built to keep that with you.

  • Nothing leaves your MacNo account, no cloud, no analytics, not even update checks. Your days are in a folder only you can read.
  • Open sourceEvery line is on GitHub, and the app is built from exactly that code.
  • Checked downloadThe installer compares the download with its fingerprint before it installs anything.
  • Nobody can borrow its permissionsThe app only runs the exact tracking code it shipped with. If anything changes that file, it stops.
  • Never the private bitsIncognito windows, Safari pages, password managers and anything after ? in a web address are never recorded. Away for 3 minutes and the clock stops.
  • You stay in chargeNo admin password. Both permissions are optional and can be taken back in System Settings. One line removes it all.

Questions

Does it slow my Mac down?

No. It looks once every five seconds and uses well under 1% of one processor core and around 55 MB of memory.

Which browsers does it read?

Chrome, Brave, Edge and Vivaldi: the site, the path and the tab title. Safari and Firefox count as app time only, because their private windows cannot be told apart from normal ones.

How do I add my clients?

Open ~/Library/Application Support/RM Activity/settings.json and add them to the list. Changes apply to old days too.

"clients": {
  "list": [
    {"name": "Acme Dental", "aliases": ["acme"], "domains": ["acmedental.com"]},
    {"name": "Northwind Plumbing", "domains": ["northwind.co"]}
  ]
}
Why Terminal and not a normal download?

RM Activity is not signed by Apple, because we are not a registered Apple developer. Installing from Terminal avoids the "unidentified developer" warning, and the installer checks the download's fingerprint itself.

How do I update it?

Run the install line again. Your settings and your recorded days stay.

How do I remove it?

Run this in Terminal. Your recorded days stay in their folder until you delete it.

curl -fsSL https://rmmarketingmcc.github.io/rm-activity/install.sh | bash -s -- --uninstall
Something does not work

Choose Report a problem in the app, or the button below. It opens a report on GitHub with the version and macOS filled in, never your days or your clients. It needs a free GitHub account.
